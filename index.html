Know the room

Assessment at a glance

Short, individual, face-to-face, and professional from the moment you arrive.

20%Interview assessment

Week 8 · 3–5 minutes · audio recorded

  • 01
    Two mandatory questions

    “Tell me about yourself” and a study skill linked to your future job.

  • 02
    Teacher-choice questions

    Self-description, behavioural, and future-focused questions.

  • 03
    Follow-up questions

    Be ready to explain, give a true example, or say an idea in a different way.

  • 04
    No reading

    Prepare thoroughly, but answer naturally without notes or a screen.

How marks are earned

Your success compass

Depth wins marks: say it, show it with a real example, then explain why it matters.

40%

Content

  • Answer the question you hear
  • Give true, detailed examples
  • Listen actively
  • Sound prepared, not memorised
10%

Professionalism

  • Arrive on time
  • Dress appropriately
  • Use polite language
  • Treat it like a real interview
25%

Language

  • Use course vocabulary and grammar
  • Keep talking clearly
  • Correct small mistakes calmly
  • Manage hesitation
25%

Communication

  • Use a clear voice and pauses
  • Maintain eye contact and posture
  • Use natural gestures
  • Recover confidence without notes
